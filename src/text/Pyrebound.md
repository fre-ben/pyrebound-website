# elevator pitch
**Pyrebound** is a **top-down exploration adventure** game in which the player is always trying to move further into the darkness by burning **pyres made from corpses of the undead** in an  unconventional, **non-stationary tower defense**-style loop.
# value prop
The game offers a **strategic experience** about constantly **balancing safety, defense, offense and mobility** in order to be able to **explore** the world covered by pitch-black, **opaque darkness until lit** by the pyres.
# emotional core
At Pyrebound’s heart is a simple and hopeful message: “Light can be found even in the darkest of all hours.”
# summary
Embody a mythological fire spirit tasked with fighting against the ever-encroaching Darkness and rekindling hope in the darkest of all hours.

Explore the remnants of an ancient world while constantly driven to restless flight by the darkness and the desiccated undead. Survive to find the lost embers of inspiration and relight the beacons, then return to safety or confront the grand shadows of the world.

Build, burn and sustain magical pyres made of the corpses of the Desiccated that hunt you to push away the darkness for a brief time. Keep moving and slowly uncover a mysterious history of ancient technology and crude sorcery.
# structure
## hub
Get to know the cast of survivors in the Glimmering Glade. Bring them hope, so despair may give way to courage and heart. (hub area with interactable NPCs, providing lore, progression & narrative)
## exploration
Explore four hand-crafted areas drenched in darkness. Slay the Desiccated to build pyres and uncover what’s hidden. With enough embers, light the beacons to summon the shadows. (progressively uncovered levels, main game stage, exploration, challenge and bosses)
## finale
After all beacons are lit, one final area awaits. Summon all hope, any who enter here. (smaller, more intense final stage with the final boss fight, hopeful survivors help)
# gameplay
## level areas
Exploratory tower defense
- push forward by building pyres along the way
- once lit areas stay partially translucent (exploration)
- find resources, artefacts and secrets in the dark
Light the beacons
- find enough embers to activate the beacons
- prevail through the challenging rituals
- either return to safety or face the area boss
## enemies and pyres
Enemies are danger and resource combined  
- fight for survival = fight for resources = ability to move on
- balance stationary defense, careful offense and exploration
- player’s fighting abilities are limited, pyres fight for you
The loop  
1. enemies attack, kill enemies
2. throw the corpses into a pile before they reanimate
3. choose a pyre type to kindle
4. while the pyre is burning it fights for you
5. sustain pyres with additional corpses
